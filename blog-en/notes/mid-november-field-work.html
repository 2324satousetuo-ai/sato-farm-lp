Vegetables Fields November 15, 2026

Mid-November Field Work | What Is Left After Planting

It is already mid-November, and we still have plenty of work to do in the fields.

Finished Work

  • Onions: We finished planting them. We sowed three bags of seeds, and about one bag's worth of seedlings was left over. Almost every seed sprouted, so I think we did a great job growing them! We plan to make the seedbed bigger and plant the extra seedlings.
  • Garlic: We finished planting it. I want to plant a little more, but it is almost too late in the season. I am not sure how much more we can do.
  • Cabbage & Broccoli: We finished planting the seedlings we bought, and they are growing well—10 cabbages and 15 broccoli plants.

Work in Progress

  • Sweet Potatoes (Beniharuka): We are digging them up right now, but it is taking a long time. Once we finish, we will let them rest for a while to make them sweeter.
  • Green Beans: We are harvesting them now. We boil and eat the pods, though the beans inside are getting ripe and a bit hard.

Coming Up Next

  • Shimonita Green Onions: We still need to replant them. We are a little behind schedule.
  • Spinach: We need to hurry and sow the seeds. We are a bit behind on this, too.

What We Skipped This Year

It got too late in the season, so we decided not to sow seeds for garland chrysanthemum (shungiku), Japanese mustard spinach (komatsuna), and kakina (a kind of rapeseed green) this year.

About Daikon Radishes

Our daikon radishes are growing nicely. However, I wish we had planted a little more. Back then, I thought it was too late and stopped sowing, so I regret not planting a bit more.

Finding the best time to sow seeds is difficult every year.

Next year, I plan to sow more seeds earlier in the autumn.
