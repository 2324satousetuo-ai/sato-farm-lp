Random Thoughts July 9, 2026

Lost in the maze of mobile display.

On PC, it looks perfect. Yet on a smartphone, it falls apart mercilessly. Fix, check, fix, check—caught in an endless loop, I've found myself despairing, wondering if it's hopeless.

What keeps me going through this heart-breaking challenge is my partner: AI tools. After dozens of unreasonable requests, they never complain—their patience leaves me nothing but grateful.

And yet, that is precisely where the biggest wall stands. How to convey my intentions to AI clearly and accurately—that is the hard part.

Today again, I stare at the screen, tinkering here and there through days of struggle. But what beautiful view awaits beyond this uphill battle? Please stay with me to see how this puzzle ends—a senior's pride is on the line.
