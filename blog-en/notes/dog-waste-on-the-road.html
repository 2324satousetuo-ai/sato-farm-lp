Dog Waste on the Roadside Again — Please Be Responsible and Take It Home

This morning, I stepped outside and found dog waste on the roadside, right behind my car in our parking area.

"Who did this? Again!"

The words came out before I could stop myself. If your dog leaves waste behind, please take responsibility. Pick it up and take it home.

I think this problem is over, but then it happens again. Today was no different. Along the farm roads near our fields, the situation is even worse. Some people even let their dogs run loose in our fields. It is hard to believe. Although fewer people leave dog waste along the roads than in the past, the problem has not gone away.

The Owner's Duty

Having a dog means more than simply taking care of its life. It also means taking responsibility for its waste.

When you take your dog for a walk, bring a bag and a scoop. Pick up the waste and take it home. This is a basic rule and the very least every dog owner should do.

If you cannot clean up after your dog, you should not have one. The way an owner behaves says a lot about their character.

Health and Hygiene

Dog waste left on the ground is not just a problem because of its appearance or smell. It may contain parasites and harmful bacteria. When it dries, some of it can turn into tiny particles and become airborne. It can also stick to the soles of our shoes and the tires of our cars, and then be carried into our homes.

This is especially concerning on roads used by children walking to school.

We grow rice and vegetables in our fields. Soil is where the food we eat is grown. As farmers who work hard to produce safe and healthy food, we cannot ignore animal waste left near our farmland.

The Environment and Our Scenery

When it rains, dog waste can be washed into roadside ditches, then into irrigation channels, and eventually into rivers. Unpleasant smells and flies are also a problem.

We are lucky to live among beautiful mountains and rice fields, where golden ears of rice sway in the wind. The scenery changes with every season. These are precious parts of our local community that people have cared for over many years.

Even one pile of dog waste on the roadside can spoil this beautiful scenery.

Trust in Our Community

When we cannot identify the person who left the waste behind, everyone walking a dog may be viewed with suspicion.

These days, more and more people enjoy walking their lovely dogs. Most of them are probably responsible owners who follow the rules. However, the careless behavior of just one person can make even responsible dog owners feel uncomfortable. It damages trust among neighbors and affects the atmosphere of the whole community.

Dogs should bring people together, not create problems between them. A friendly "Good morning!" during a morning walk is one of the things that makes our town special.

To protect this friendly atmosphere, every dog owner needs to do their part.

I Decided to Put Up Warning Signs

I would like to speak directly to the person who leaves dog waste behind and walks away as if nothing has happened. However, it is difficult to catch someone doing this.

So this time, I decided to make some clear warning signs and put them up in two or three places around my house.

I started the morning in a bad mood. But I decided to change my attitude and put my frustration into words. I wanted to create a clear message that would help prevent this problem from happening again.

THIS IS A NUISANCE!

PLEASE DO NOT LEAVE DOG WASTE HERE!

PICK IT UP AND TAKE IT HOME!
