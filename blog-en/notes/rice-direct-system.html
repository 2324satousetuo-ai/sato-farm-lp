Random Thoughts August 17, 2026

What Sato Farm Is Building

Right now, Sato Farm is building something new.

It is not just an online shop.

Our rice harvest is limited. So we sell our rice based on how much we can harvest each year.

First, people who are interested can sign up as members.

They can choose one of three options:

“I really want to buy.”
“It depends on the price.”
“I just want to see how things go.”

It is easy and simple to join.

The price is not fixed from the start.

We look at the cost of growing the rice, the amount we can harvest, and how the rice grows that year.

First, we share a reference price. Then, as the harvest gets closer, we share an estimated price. After the harvest, we decide the final price.

We work with our members to find a price that feels fair to both sides.

The price is not simply decided by us. It is shaped through conversation.

Some years, we may not grow enough rice for everyone who wants to buy it.

When that happens, we ask trusted neighboring farmers for help.

One small farm cannot always meet the demand alone. Working with our neighbors is an important part of this system.

We share updates mainly through LINE.

We tell you about planting, how the rice is growing, and our estimate of the harvest.

We want you to follow the story of the rice as it grows.

Before we talk about price, we want you to know what is happening in the fields.

Right now, we are just getting started with member registration and confirmation emails.

We are building this system step by step, testing as we go.

No rush. One step at a time.

This project is not only about selling rice.

It is about sharing our harvest with people who care, and learning from each year to make the next one better.

That is the cycle Sato Farm hopes to build.
