Skiing Agatsuma October 16, 2026

Am I Letting Go of My Blog, or Is Time Doing the Job?

I love traveling, and before I know it, I’m off somewhere again.

This year alone, I have visited Mishima and Odawara in February, Takamatsu and Shodoshima in June, and Kobe, Himeji, and Kinosaki Onsen in October. And in November, I will head to Kyushu for the sixth year in a row. Every year, I attend a regular meeting in Kokura, Fukuoka Prefecture. This year marks the 32nd time the meeting has been held. If I were to cut things out of my life, this meeting would probably be one of the first to go. But somehow, I have kept it going all these years. But somehow, it has lasted this long.

In December, the long-awaited ski season will begin. Last winter, I spent about 80 days at the ski resorts, from mid-December to early April. To be honest, I hardly have time to update my blog while working on the slopes. At this rate, the blog itself may be the first thing I clear out.
