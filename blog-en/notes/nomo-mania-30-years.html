30 Years as a Nomo Mania

Japanese Players Making History in MLB

I have been enjoying MLB since 1995, the year Hideo Nomo made his debut. To be exact, I was interested in MLB ever since I learned about Masanori Murakami, the first Japanese MLB player who debuted in 1964. However, after Nomo started playing, TV broadcasts on NHK BS really took off, and many passionate fans like me became true "Nomo Maniacs."

If I pick the four greatest Japanese MLB players from my point of view, they are Nomo, Ichiro, Hideki Matsui, and Shohei Ohtani.

Key Japanese MLB Players by Era and Style

The Pioneer Pitchers

  • Hideo Nomo: The pioneer who opened the door for Japanese players with his unique "Tornado" style. He pitched two no-hitters.
  • Hideki Irabu, Kazuhiro Sasaki, Shigetoshi Hasegawa, Masato Yoshii

Pioneer Hitters

  • Ichiro Suzuki: An incredible hitter who won both Rookie of the Year and MVP in the same season, and set a historic record with 262 hits in a single season.
  • Tsuyoshi Shinjo, Hideki Matsui, Kazuo Matsui, Kenji Johjima, Tadahito Iguchi, So Taguchi

Star Pitchers of the 2000s–2010s

  • Daisuke Matsuzaka, Hiroki Kuroda, Koji Uehara, Hisashi Iwakuma, Yu Darvish, Masahiro Tanaka, Kenta Maeda

The Current Generation

  • Shohei Ohtani: A one-of-a-kind star who keeps making history as both a pitcher and a hitter ("two-way player").
  • Yusei Kikuchi, Seiya Suzuki, Masataka Yoshida, Kodai Senga, Shota Imanaga, Yoshinobu Yamamoto, Roki Sasaki, Kazuma Okamoto, Munetaka Murakami

The path Nomo opened up by himself has now become a stage where Japanese players challenge themselves as key team members every year. I cannot wait to see what they will do next!
