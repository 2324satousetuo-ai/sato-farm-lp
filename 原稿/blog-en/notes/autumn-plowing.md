# Autumn Plowing: The First Step Toward Next Year's Rice

Rice　October 25, 2026
**October 4, 2026**

The rice harvest is over.

**October 14, 2026**

Autumn plowing is complete.

Before plowing the field, I spread well-rotted chicken manure over the chopped rice straw. This helps the straw break down and prepares the soil for next year's rice.

This time, I plowed a little deeper than usual. I plan to plow the field about twice more before spring. The next two times, I will plow more lightly.

With the harvest and autumn plowing finished, the main work in this year's rice fields has come to a close.

The fields are already getting ready for next year's rice.
