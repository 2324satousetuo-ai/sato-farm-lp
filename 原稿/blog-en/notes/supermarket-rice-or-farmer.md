# Supermarket Rice or Rice from a Farmer? Why Not Try a Different Way?

Random Thoughts　August 14, 2026

Rice is an important part of our daily meals.

Most people buy rice at a supermarket.
They choose the rice they like and buy it when they need it.

That is the normal way today.

But there is another choice.

**You can buy brown rice directly from a farmer.**

This shorter distance can mean more than you might think.

## A Shorter Journey

Rice at a supermarket takes a long journey:

Farmer → Collector → Wholesaler → Supermarket

Rice is collected, stored, transported, polished, and put on the shelves.

These are all important jobs.
But they also add costs, such as transportation, storage, and labor.

When you buy directly from a farmer, the journey becomes shorter.

**Who grew the rice?
What kind of field did it grow in?
Where does your money go?**

You can see these things a little more clearly.

## Rice with a Story

The value of farm-direct rice is not only about price.

The summer was very hot.
There was little rain.
A typhoon came.

These things are part of the farmer's year.

When you know the story behind the rice, your bowl of rice becomes more than **“just a product.”**

It becomes food with a story.

## Choosing Brown Rice

You can also buy the rice as brown rice.

Then you can polish only the amount you need.

You can use a rice polisher at home or a coin-operated machine nearby.

Freshly polished rice has a wonderful smell and gentle sweetness.

## A Little More Work, but More Meaning

Buying directly from a farmer takes a little more work.

The farmer must store, pack, and send the rice.
The customer may need to think about shipping, storage, and polishing the rice.

**It takes a little more time and effort.**

But that effort has meaning.

**It is one way to support the farmer who grows your food.**

You can see the farmer.
You can see the story behind the rice.
And perhaps, you can support each other.

## More Choices, Richer Meals

Supermarket rice and farm-direct rice are both good choices.

The important thing is to know the difference and choose what is right for you.

Why not try it once, with a small amount?

If the rice arrives as brown rice, take it to a nearby rice polishing machine.

Then cook it and enjoy it.

**Your usual bowl of rice may look just a little different.**
