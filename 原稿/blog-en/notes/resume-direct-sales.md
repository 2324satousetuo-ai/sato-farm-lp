# Announcement: We Are Resuming Our Direct Sales

Rice　September 13, 2026
A while ago, we made a difficult decision.
We decided to postpone the launch of our direct sales system for a while.

The weather had been unsettled, and we wanted to wait until we could feel confident about the quality of this year's rice.
We also felt that our announcement had come a little too early, before our preparations were fully in place.

To be honest, we also had some quiet worries about things beyond the direct sales system itself.
Would our computer work properly when we needed it?
Would our printer cause problems at an important moment?

But now, the rice plants are moving toward harvest, and the grains are beginning to fill.
At the same time, we have done our best to prepare the direct sales system.
The whole process is now ready: member registration, price information, order reception, and shipping.

We have come this far.
Now, there is only one thing to do: move forward.

With the rice harvest coming at the end of this month, we are pleased to announce that we are once again accepting direct sales registrations.

If you are interested in seeing how this year's rice has grown, we would be very happy to have you join us by registering as a member.

Thank you for your continued support.

Sato Farms
